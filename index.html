Simulation Rollouts — RoboTwin Benchmark

BiGeo is evaluated on 12 bimanual manipulation tasks from the RoboTwin benchmark. Clips below play automatically as they scroll into view (real-time, 1× speed). For tasks executable by either arm, both left- and right-arm episodes are included in the repository.

BlockHammerBeatGrasp the hammer and strike the block, using the arm on the block's side.
BlockHandoverHand the block over from one arm to the other, then place it on the target pad.
BlockStackTwoStack two cubes precisely at the target location.
BlockStackThreeLong-horizon stacking of three cubes using both arms.
ContainerPlacePick up the container and place it onto the target plate.
DiverseBottlesPickBoth arms simultaneously lift two bottles of diverse shapes to target poses.
DualShoesPlacePlace a pair of shoes side by side, one per arm, with correct orientation.
EmptyCupPlacePick the empty cup and set it down steadily on the coaster.
PickAppleMessyPick the apple out of a cluttered scene with distractors.
PutAppleCabinetOne arm opens the cabinet while the other places the apple inside.
ShoePlacePlace a single shoe on the target mat with the correct heading.
ToolAdjustGrasp the tool and adjust it to the required pose.
Every trial shown on this page is closed-loop policy execution. Success markers in the supplementary video are annotated post-hoc for readability only. This page contains no author- or institution-identifying information.
